Phase H1 -- dceLive on the compiler corpus
==========================================

Machine: macOS 26, arm64, Nim 2.2.10. Branch jit/h1 off fast-devloop 55c0521e.
Corpus: a cold `nimony n src/nimony/nimony.nim`, 131 modules, 131 `.dce.nif`
analyses totalling 3.1 MB. Pin: nativenif 7b838ec.

"base" is a hexer built from 55c0521e; "head" is the same tree with the
`markLive` fix (d40594ce). Everything else in the toolchain is identical
between the two sides -- only `bin/hexer` is swapped -- so every number below
isolates that one change.

4. Wall, per round -- and why the A/B median does not move
----------------------------------------------------------

`bench/devloop_ab.sh` runs the scenario 5 times and reports the MEDIAN. For
`self.editbody` that median does not measure dceLive at all: the edit inserts
`if isNewScope: discard $i`, which adds no symbol and no use-edge, so after
round 0 the module's `.dce.nif` comes out byte-identical (P0c's sorted
serialization) and the dceLive node is never woken again. With --report:

  head round 0   nifmake-report arkham=3 dceEmit=1 dceLive=1 hexer=1 link=1 total=7
  head round 1   nifmake-report arkham=3 dceEmit=1            hexer=1 link=1 total=6
  head round 2   nifmake-report arkham=3 dceEmit=1            hexer=1 link=1 total=6
  head round 3   nifmake-report arkham=3 dceEmit=1            hexer=1 link=1 total=6
  head round 4   nifmake-report arkham=3 dceEmit=1            hexer=1 link=1 total=6

So 4 of the 5 rounds skip the stage this phase changes, and the median is the
steady state. Per-round wall, fork-point sources, two interleaved pairs:

  base  round 0 1.38   round 1 1.04  round 2 1.02  round 3 1.04
  head  round 0 1.09   round 1 1.04  round 2 1.04  round 3 1.03
  base2 round 0 1.40   round 1 1.04  round 2 1.05  round 3 1.05
  head2 round 0 1.10   round 1 1.04  round 2 1.04  round 3 1.04

  first rebuild after an edit (dceLive runs):  1.39 -> 1.10  (-0.29 s, 1.26x)
  steady state (dceLive skipped):              1.04 -> 1.04  (unchanged)

The -0.29 s is the dceLive delta from section 3, landing where it should.
