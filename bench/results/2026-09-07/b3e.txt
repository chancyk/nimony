"module level" carries this phase's own cost: the context digest, and on a warm
run reading the previous 10.7 MB .asm.nif back and hashing it.  "serialize" on a
warm run is the splice (one memcpy per declaration), the rebuilt index and the
sidecar write.

which is F2's three changed lowering-output declarations, exactly.
