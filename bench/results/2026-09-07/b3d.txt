1. Why 627 did not become 3 on its own
--------------------------------------

Declaration-level diff of the two .asm.nif corpora across the same edit.  The
asm-NIF carries NO line information (grep -c '@' over all 54 MB is 0), so a
declaration's raw bytes are its identity and the embedded (.index ...) gives
the ranges.

  before this branch:  semygjvq21.asm.nif: 974 decls, changed 434
                       TOTAL: 7660 decls, changed 434  (only sem's file differs)

Masking one arkham name family at a time:

  mask none                       changed  434 of 974
  mask `L<n>                      changed  255 of 974
  mask + `aggtmp<n> / `nctmp<n>   changed    3 of 974

  after the arkham commit:  semygjvq21.asm.nif: 974 decls, changed 3
                            TOTAL: 7660 decls, changed 3

The 3 are semStmt and the two procs intramodinliner splices it into -- exactly
what notes/f2.md's sidecar reports for hexer's output.

  after,  warm again       ac8aac92f3459605
  before again, from mixed 94a2c53961d8ef63    <- blob holds BOTH versions

The eighth line is the new hazard: after this phase a blob file can hold
fragments recorded from two different versions of one module, and each has to
answer for itself.


4. arkham's own time, and what a per-proc splice could reach
------------------------------------------------------------

A temporary probe around generateA64Buf (built, measured, reverted), arkham
over semygjvq21.c.nif, median of two:

  [arkham probe] collect 1 ms, procs 139 ms (526 procs), rest 0 ms
  total process time: 196 ms

  139 ms  per-proc emission          <- what a splice removes
   56 ms  parse of the 4.64 MB .c.nif + serialize of the 10.74 MB .asm.nif
    1 ms  collect (the whole-module program model)

Reading back the previous .asm.nif for the 523 unchanged procs costs ~30 ms at
loadParse's measured 333 MB/s unless the cache is stored as `bif`.  So the
splice lands arkham near 90-110 ms, not the 50 ms the gate asks for; reaching
50 ms needs the parse and the serialize to become incremental too.
