3. Byte churn of the same edit, and why it does not move as far
---------------------------------------------------------------

The same three artifacts split TEXTUALLY into the children of their root
`(stmts ...)` -- the method of `notes/b3b.md` section 3, which keeps line info:

  artifact     before          after
  .x.nif       1148 of 1753    525 of 1753
  .c.nif        632 of  974    452 of  974
  .asm.nif      460 of  975    434 of  975

These are dominated by something F2 does not own: an inserted LINE renumbers
every `@...` below it in the file, so every declaration after the edit point
differs textually whatever its symbols are called.  That is why F1 built a
line-info-blind digest, and why section 1's 3 -- not this table's 525 -- is
the number a declaration-level splice consults.

`.asm.nif` moves least because arkham names its own temps from the ABSOLUTE
TOKEN POSITION in its input (`aggtmp<pos>`, `nctmp<pos>`, `botmp<pos>`, ...),
so any size change upstream renames them.  That is section 14's step 5 and it
lives in nativenif, not here.


4. nifasm's blob cache on the same edit (NIFASM_PROFILE=1)
----------------------------------------------------------

  before:  blobHits 3415   blobStale 18   blobRecorded 627
  after:   blobHits 3415   blobStale 18   blobRecorded 627

Identical, and that is the expected result rather than a disappointment.
`blobcache.sameSource` validates a fragment against its MODULE's `.asm.nif`
stamp, so sem's file changing at all still drops every fragment whose symbol
lives in sem, and every fragment elsewhere that references one.  F2 changes
how MUCH of that file changes, not how the check is made.  `notes/b3b.md`
section 12 priced per-symbol validity at 627 -> ~467 before this phase and
~2 after it; this is the measurement that says the second half of that is now
available, i.e. step 4 is worth ~180 ms of a 1.28 s loop instead of ~55 ms.
