  Cold is unchanged, as expected: nothing is memoized yet and the time is the

  were first-launch taxes on binaries that had just been linked.)

B. bench/ctfe_bench.nim — 14 sub-programs, via bench/ctfe_latency.sh
================================================================================

  scenario                          baseline      p0a      ratio
  ---------------------------------------------------------------
  cold                                6.97 s     6.99 s      1.0x
  warm                                0.017 s    0.017 s     1.0x
  edit (append a statement)           0.48 s     0.14 s      3.4x
  forced (-f)                        10.06 s     1.54 s      6.5x

raw (rounds 2 and 3; round 1 discarded, see caveat):
  baseline  cold 6.641 6.972 | warm 0.021 0.017 | edit 0.514 0.443 | -f 10.327 9.789
  p0a       cold 6.950 6.989 | warm 0.017 0.017 | edit 0.141 0.137 | -f  1.535 1.537

  Per evaluation, from the edit scenario: baseline (0.48 - 0.05 backend) / 14
  = 31 ms; p0a (0.14 - 0.05) / 14 = 6 ms, and the 6 ms is nifler + nimsem +
  the backend of the one edited module, not the evaluations — the memo serves
  all fourteen with no process at all.

  `--report` under `-f`, p0a:
    nifmake-report nifler=32 nimsem=6 total=38
    nifmake-report cc=6 dceEmit=6 dceLive=1 hexer=6 lengc=6 link=1 total=26
  i.e. the outer graph is rebuilt (that is what `-f` asks for) and no
  sub-program's build nodes run. Baseline ran the same outer graph and
  additionally deleted and rebuilt ~30 nodes per const inside 14 `nimony s`
  children, which is the 10 s.
