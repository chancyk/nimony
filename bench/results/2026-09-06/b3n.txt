== what changed, in one paragraph ==========================================

`deps.blobCacheDir(config)` is `<nimcache>/blobcache`, beside `ocache/` and
`ccache/`.  The native `link` node passes it to nifasm as `--blobcache:<dir>`
and `nimony.runProject` hands the same string to `engine.runWholeProgram`,
which calls `AsmSession.useBlobCache` before `declare` and `saveBlobCache`
after `finishCode` -- before the guest runs, because a guest is free never to
return.  `--no-blobcache` and `NIMONY_BLOBCACHE=off` clear it; unlike
`NIMONY_CCACHE` this one does change the `*.build.nif`, because the flag it
suppresses is part of the `link` node's command line.  `--verbose` adds
`emitRoots=` and `blobcache=on hits=/stale=/recorded=` to the `[run-engine]`
line; `--profile` (and `--verbose`) additionally turns on nifasm's own
per-stage table.  The `intercept("__exit")` workaround B1 carried in
`engine.nim` is gone: the bug is fixed upstream in nativenif f8d2676.
